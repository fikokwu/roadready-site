1. Not official

RoadReady is made by Endless Technologies. No government, ministry or licensing agency made it, checked it or approved it. That includes the Ontario Ministry of Transportation and DriveTest, Alberta Transportation and Alberta registry agents, ICBC in British Columbia, and every other provincial and territorial licensing body, such as SGI, MPI and the SAAQ.

We only use their names to say which test we help you study for. Their trademarks belong to them.

We write our lessons and questions in our own words, from public official sources. They are study material. The official handbook and the law are the real rules, and if we ever differ from them, they win.

2. No promise you'll pass

RoadReady is practice. We can't promise you'll pass any test. Our mock exams follow each province's published test format, but they aren't the real test. The licensing authority sets the real questions, format and pass mark, and can change them at any time. Where a province hasn't published a detail, we use our best estimate and say so.

3. Mistakes

We work hard to keep everything accurate and up to date, but rules change and mistakes happen. We can't promise the content is complete, current or error-free. Check anything important against your province's official handbook, and never rely on the app for a real decision on the road.

Found a mistake? Tell us at support@beaconsec.ca and we'll fix it quickly.
