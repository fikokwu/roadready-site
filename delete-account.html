Delete your account

You can delete your RoadReady account, and everything in it, at any time.

Contents

  1. In the app
  2. Without the app
  3. What gets deleted
  4. What's kept

In the app

  1. Open RoadReady and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email support@beaconsec.ca from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my RoadReady account". We'll delete it within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your email address, name and sign-in details.
  • All the progress saved with it: XP, streaks, study days, lessons, answers and mock exam results.

What's kept

Nothing linked to you. Backup copies are gone within 30 days. Progress saved on your own phone stays there until you reset it in the app or delete the app.
