Delete your RoadReady account

RoadReady is published by Endless Technologies. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open RoadReady and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately.

Without the app

Email support@roadready.beaconsec.ca from the address your account uses, with the subject “Delete my RoadReady account”. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name and sign-in details.
  • All study progress stored with your account: XP, streaks, study days, lessons read, per-question answer history and mock exam results.

What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Copies in encrypted backups expire within 30 days. Progress saved on your own phone stays there until you reset it in the app or uninstall the app.
