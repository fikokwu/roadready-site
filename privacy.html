Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026
Applies to: RoadReady for iOS and Android
Published by: Endless Technologies
Contact: support@beaconsec.ca

The short version. RoadReady works offline and you never need an account to study. Without one, your progress stays on your device. If you create an account, we store your sign-in details and your study progress on our servers so it syncs between your devices and survives a lost phone. You can delete your account, and everything attached to it, from inside the app at any time. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

What we collect

If you use RoadReady without an account (the default)

Nothing leaves your device. We collect no personal information at all. Your progress ( experience points, streaks, which lessons you have opened or completed, your per-question results, your best exam scores, your chosen province, your daily goal, and your test date if you set one) is stored only in your device's local storage. We cannot see it. If you delete the app, it is gone.

Your province

RoadReady needs to know which province's rules to teach you. You can pick your province from a list, or let the app detect it.

If you choose detection, the app asks your device for a low-accuracy, city-level location, converts it to a province, and keeps only the two-letter province code (for example, “ON”). The coordinates are used in memory for that single lookup and are never stored, never transmitted to us, and never logged. We never learn your address, your city, or where you are.

The conversion from coordinates to a province name is performed by your device's own operating system geocoding service (Apple or Google, depending on your phone). That lookup is governed by your device maker's privacy policy, not ours.

You can decline the location permission. The app works identically. You just pick your province from a list instead. You can change or revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.

If you create an account (optional)

Accounts are optional. They exist so your progress syncs between your devices and is not lost if you change or lose your phone.

If you create one, we collect:

DataWhy
Email addressTo identify your account, sign you in, and send password resets
PasswordOnly if you sign up with email. Stored only as a cryptographic hash, never as readable text
Display nameShown in the app so it can greet you; you choose it, or it comes from Apple or Google

You can also sign in with Apple or Google instead of a password. If you do, that company confirms who you are and shares your name, your email address and an account ID with us; Google may also share a link to your profile picture, which we do not use. With Sign in with Apple you can choose “Hide My Email”, and we then receive only a private relay address that forwards to you. We never see your Apple or Google password.

Progress sync

When you are signed in, the app uploads the same study progress described above and keeps it in step across your devices, so it can be restored if you change or lose your phone. That data is linked to your account. It is about your studying (scores, streaks, lesson and question records) and contains no free text, no contacts, and no location.

What we never collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no ads of any kind
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera, calendar, or health data
  • No precise or stored location
  • No payment details: version 1.0 is free, and if paid features arrive later, Apple and Google process the payment and we never see your card

Who else is involved

Supabase provides our authentication and database hosting. They act as our data processor: they store account and progress data on our behalf and may not use it for their own purposes. Our database is hosted in Canada (Amazon Web Services, Canada Central region).

Resend delivers the emails an account needs: the message that confirms your email address and the link to reset your password. To do that it processes your email address and the content of those messages, and it may do so outside Canada. We do not send marketing email.

Apple and Google operate the app stores, the device geocoding service, and Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google if you choose to use them. Their handling of your data is governed by their own policies.

We use no other third parties. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep things

Local progress stays on your device until you reset it in the app or delete the app.

Account data is kept while your account exists. If you delete your account in the app, your account record and all progress associated with it are deleted immediately. Copies in encrypted backups expire within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset your progress. The app's profile screen clears your progress on the device and, if you are signed in, the copy held on our servers.
  • Study without an account. You never have to create one.
  • Delete your account. In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account record and all progress stored with it. If you no longer have the app, see how to delete your account.
  • Access, correct, or export your data. Email us at the address above and we will respond within 30 days, as PIPEDA requires.
  • Withdraw location permission at any time in device settings.

If you are in Canada and are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. If you are in Quebec, Law 25 gives you additional rights, including data portability.

Children

RoadReady is intended for people studying for a driver's knowledge test, those of age to begin the driver licensing program in their province. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app works fully without an account, a younger user can use it without giving us any information at all. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for any change that materially affects you, tell you in the app before it takes effect.
