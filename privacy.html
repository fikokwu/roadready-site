The short version

  • You can study without an account. Then nothing leaves your phone.
  • An account is optional. It backs up your progress and syncs it between your devices.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We never sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your account at any time, right in the app.

Without an account

Your progress stays on your phone. That includes your XP, streaks, lessons, quiz and exam results, your province, your daily goal and your test date. We can't see any of it. If you delete the app, it's gone.

Your province

RoadReady needs your province to teach you the right rules. You can pick it from a list, or let the app find it.

If you let the app find it, your phone looks up your rough location (city level) and turns it into a province. The app keeps only the two-letter code, like "ON". Your location is never saved, never sent to us and never logged. The lookup is done by your phone's own service from Apple or Google.

You can say no to location access. You'll just pick your province from the list instead, and you can change your mind any time in your phone's settings.

With an account

Accounts are optional. They back up your progress and keep it the same on all your devices, so it isn't lost if you change or lose your phone. If you make one, we store:

WhatWhy
Email addressTo sign you in and send account emails, like a password reset
PasswordOnly if you sign up with email. It's stored scrambled (hashed), so no one can read it
NameSo the app can greet you. You choose it, or it comes from Apple or Google
Study progressSo it's backed up and synced. It's only about your studying: no messages, contacts or location

Signing in with Apple or Google

If you sign in with Apple or Google, that company tells us your name, your email address and an account ID. Google may also send a link to your profile picture, which we don't use. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email", we only get a private address that forwards to you. We never see your Apple or Google password.

What we never collect

  • Ads or advertising IDs
  • Analytics or tracking
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar or health data
  • Your exact location
  • Payment details. The app is free. If we add paid features later, Apple or Google take the payment and we never see your card.

Who helps us

  • Supabase stores accounts and progress for us, on servers in Canada (Amazon Web Services, Canada Central). They can't use your data for anything else.
  • Resend sends account emails: the one that confirms your email address and the password reset link. It handles your email address to do this, and may do so outside Canada. We don't send marketing emails.
  • Apple and Google run the app stores, your phone's location lookup, and Sign in with Apple and Google if you use them. Their own privacy policies apply.

That's everyone. We don't sell, rent or trade your data, and we don't share it for ads.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you reset your progress in the app or delete the app.
  • With an account: for as long as you have the account. When you delete it, your account and progress are deleted right away. Backup copies are gone within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Reset your progress from Profile in the app. If you're signed in, this clears it on our servers too.
  • Delete your account from Profile, then Delete account. No app? Here's how to delete it.
  • See, fix or get a copy of your data by emailing us. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • Turn off location access any time in your phone's settings.

If you're not happy with how we handled a privacy request, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

RoadReady is for people studying for their driver's knowledge test: those of age to begin the driver licensing program in their province. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. Anyone can use the app without an account and share nothing at all. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects you in a big way, we'll tell you in the app before it happens.
